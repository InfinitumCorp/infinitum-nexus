Windows

Desktop app for Windows 10 and 11. Opens mailto: links and shows new mail on the taskbar.

Download for Windows ARM64

First time opening it

If Windows SmartScreen says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. The installer sets up Cumulus Mail for your account only and needs no admin rights.

macOS

Desktop app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 12 or later.

Download for Mac .zip

First time opening it

Open the .dmg and drag Cumulus Mail to Applications. If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Linux

AppImage runs on most distributions. The .deb is for Debian, Ubuntu and Mint.

AppImage (x64) .deb (x64)
AppImage (ARM64) .deb (ARM64)

Installing

AppImage: make it executable (chmod +x CumulusMail-*.AppImage) and run it. Debian or Ubuntu: sudo apt install ./CumulusMail-*.deb.

Android

For Android 8 or later. Opens your mail in a fast, full-screen window and keeps you signed in with your passkey.

Download APK

Installing

Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then choose Install. Updates install the same way.

iPhone & iPad

Add Cumulus://Mail to your Home Screen. It opens full screen like an app and signs in with Face ID or Touch ID.

  1. Open mail.cumuluscloud.app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Cumulus Mail is now on your Home Screen.
Open in Safari

Web

Use Cumulus://Mail in any up-to-date browser, with nothing to install. In Chrome or Edge, choose Install in the address bar to give it its own window.

Open Cumulus://Mail

Good to know

  • Every app connects straight to mail.cumuluscloud.app. On networks that slow down Cloudflare, the apps try a backup address on their own.
  • Sign in with your passkey. In the desktop apps you can also choose Sign in with your browser.
  • Each file's SHA-256 is listed under its button, so you can check the download.
