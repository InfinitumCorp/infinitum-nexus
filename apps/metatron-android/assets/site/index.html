How the suite works together

ONE SCREEN

METATRON¹⁰⁰™ is your single picture, just as ODIN°™ is for a command post. Messages, money, devices, customers and news sit side by side, and your cameras and sensors join the same view.

PREDICTION ENGINE

METATRON¹⁰⁰™ builds a living simulation of the world you care about and runs it forward. Thousands of AI agents act out a scenario, so you can ask "what if?", change a variable such as a rate decision or a supply delay, and compare futures side by side. Forecasts are probabilities that support your judgment.

PERSONAL AGENTS

SCALVS.ai™ agents research, plan and draft. :\ARCHITECT\™ turns an approved plan into finished work across your apps and proves it is done. You approve anything that spends money, shares data or speaks for you.

PRIVATE COMMUNICATIONS

FabriC++™ carries private voice, text and data, and keeps you connected over mesh when the usual networks fail.

YOUR DATA, YOUR TERMS

The Nexus Context Vault™ lets what one product learns help the others, only with your permission. [HRVST]™ lets you license observations or keep them private, and earnings arrive in $FIRM Wallet™.

BLOCKCHAIN GOVERNANCE

Firmament smart contracts enforce who may see, change or approve what. Every permission you grant and every payment you make is recorded and can be reviewed or revoked.
