What VEIL can't see

Every check runs on your device. VEIL doesn't send your pages, searches, passwords, forms or downloads anywhere, keeps no browsing history, and can't see what you do in other apps or browsers.

The extension and the Android app block ad networks, trackers and data brokers by default, warn about unencrypted or look-alike sites, hold risky downloads and personal-data forms for a tap to confirm, and warn when a protected password is typed on the wrong site.
