VEILBot

VEILBot is the web crawler behind VEIL search, the private search in the VEIL browser. It reads public web pages so VEIL can find them without sending anyone to another search engine.

How to recognise it

Every request VEILBot makes carries this User-Agent:

Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; VEILBot/1.0; +https://infinitumveil.app/bot)

It runs on Cloudflare Workers, so its requests come from Cloudflare's network.

What it does, and what it never does

  • It obeys robots.txt (RFC 9309), including Crawl-delay. It re-reads it at least once a day.
  • It asks one site for at most one page at a time, never more often than every 10 seconds, and backs off when a site answers 429 or 503.
  • It obeys noindex and nofollow in robots meta tags and the X-Robots-Tag header. A noindex page is removed from VEIL's index.
  • It reads public HTML pages only. It never logs in, never fills in forms, never solves CAPTCHAs, and never tries to get around a block.
  • It skips login, account, cart and checkout pages, and links that carry session tokens.
  • VEIL search shows a short snippet and links straight to your page. Searches are never logged.

How to limit or block it

Block VEILBot from your whole site:

User-agent: VEILBot
Disallow: /

Keep it out of part of your site, or slow it down:

User-agent: VEILBot
Disallow: /private/
Crawl-delay: 30

Keep one page out of VEIL search: <meta name="robots" content="noindex">, or <meta name="veilbot" content="noindex"> for VEIL only.

Changes take effect within a day, when VEILBot next reads your robots.txt.

Remove pages or report a problem

To have pages removed from VEIL search right away, or to report VEILBot misbehaving, email veil@infinitum.corporation with your site's address. We act on removal requests as quickly as we can.
